What products does CV GADGETS sell?

Our main categories are mobile phones, laptops, tablets, iPads, MacBooks, chargers and charging accessories. Our featured brands include Apple, Samsung, HP, Dell and Lenovo. Product availability is shown in the live catalogue.

How do I place an order?

Choose an available product, review its specifications and condition, select any available configuration or colour, add it to your cart and continue to checkout. You may need to sign in before placing an order.

How do I check whether a product is available?

Products and stock quantities are managed by the store. If a product is not listed or you need a specific model or configuration, use the contact form to enquire.

What do Brand New, UK Used and Open Box mean?

Where applicable, the condition is displayed on the individual product listing. Read the product description and ask the store if you need clarification before purchasing.

How much is delivery?

Delivery availability and charges depend on the delivery location. The checkout displays the delivery options and any configured charges. Confirm the final delivery arrangement with the store before completing an order.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment methods are shown during checkout when configured by the store. Paystack is displayed only when online payment is enabled. Bank transfer and pay on delivery may be enabled by the store. Never share your PIN, password or one-time password with anyone.

How do I choose a charger?

Check the connector type, charging wattage and supported device models. A charger that physically fits a device may not support its charging requirements. Contact the store if you are unsure about compatibility.

How can I track an order?

Sign in and open your account dashboard to view your order history and any tracking details added by the store.

Can I cancel or return an order?

Sign in and open your order history to see whether a cancellation or return request is available for your order. Requests are reviewed by the store and are not automatically approved. Refer to the store's confirmed returns policy and applicable consumer rights.
