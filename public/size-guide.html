BUYING GUIDE

Know what you’re choosing.

Use these checks when comparing phones, laptops, tablets and chargers.

Phones and tablets

Compare the exact model, storage capacity, RAM, network/SIM support, screen size and battery condition where applicable. Check the listed condition and included accessories.

Laptops and MacBooks

Review the processor generation, RAM, SSD capacity, display size, graphics, operating system and battery condition. Make sure the configuration suits the apps and work you plan to use.

Product condition

Listings may be marked Brand New, UK Used, US Used, Open Box or Refurbished when applicable. Read the condition shown on the product page and ask the store if you need clarification before ordering.

Chargers and compatibility

Check the connector type, charging wattage and supported device models. A charger that fits physically may not support the power requirements of every device.

Warranty and availability

Warranty details and stock availability are shown on each listing when supplied by the store. Contact CV GADGETS if you need more information about a particular item.
