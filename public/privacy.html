YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & your choices.

This page summarises the information the website processes. CV GADGETS should complete the business contact and retention details before launch.

Information you provide

When you create an account, place an order, submit a review, contact the store or subscribe to updates, the website processes the information you submit. This may include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order details and messages. Account and order information is used to operate the store, provide customer support and maintain order history.

Accounts and shopping preferences

Account sessions use secure cookies. Cart and wishlist selections may be stored in this browser before sign-in and synchronized to your account after sign-in. You can manage account information through the customer dashboard.

Reviews and newsletters

Reviews are moderated before publication. Approved reviews may display the name, rating, message and date supplied with the review. Newsletter subscriptions require consent and include an unsubscribe mechanism.

Payments and service providers

When online payment is enabled, the selected payment provider processes the transaction. CV GADGETS does not store raw card details in this website's database. The store also uses configured hosting, database, image storage and email service providers to operate the website.

Data requests and contact

For questions or requests about your account or personal information, contact CV GADGETS. The store should publish its legal business identity, contact details, retention periods and applicable data-protection information before launch.
