ABOUT CV GADGETS

Technology for the way you live.

CV GADGETS is a Nigerian-focused electronics and gadget store built around the devices people use every day.

What we focus on

Our core categories include Apple iPhones, Samsung smartphones, HP, Dell and Lenovo laptops, Apple MacBooks, iPads, Samsung tablets, children's tablets and chargers for phones, laptops and other compatible devices.

Clear product information

Product pages are designed to show useful details such as model, storage, RAM, processor, condition, warranty and compatibility when those details are supplied for a listing.

Shopping in Nigeria

Prices are displayed in Nigerian Naira (₦). Delivery options and available payment methods are shown when configured by the store. If you need help finding a particular model, send us an enquiry.
