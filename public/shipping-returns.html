DELIVERY & RETURNS

Shipping and returns.

Delivery rates, service areas and return conditions must be confirmed by CV GADGETS before live orders are accepted.

Delivery options

The checkout supports pick-up, delivery within Lagos and delivery outside Lagos / nationwide. Actual delivery availability, fees and estimated delivery dates depend on the customer's location and must be configured by the store.

Order processing

Product availability and payment status are checked by the store. Customers can sign in to view order updates and any tracking information entered by the store.

Returns, refunds and warranty

Return eligibility, refund timelines, warranty coverage and the process for reporting a faulty or incorrect product must be published by CV GADGETS as its approved business policy. A request submitted through the account dashboard is reviewed by the store and does not automatically authorize a return or refund. Applicable statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Questions about a delivery or return

Contact the store with your order reference and the details of your enquiry.
